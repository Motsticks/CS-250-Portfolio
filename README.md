# CS 250 Portfolio

## How do I interpret user needs and implement them into a program? How does creating user stories help with this?

I interpret user needs by focusing on what the user is trying to accomplish, and which features would provide value to them. User stories break those needs into smaller, understandable requirements. They give the development team a clear idea of who the feature is for, what the user wants, and why it is important. This helps make sure the program is developed around the customer's needs instead of assumptions made by the development team.

## How do I approach developing programs? What Agile processes do I hope to incorporate into my future development work?

I approach developing programs by breaking larger problems into smaller tasks that can be completed and tested individually. Agile processes make development more flexible because teams can receive feedback and make changes throughout the project instead of waiting until the end. In my future development work, I hope to use Scrum practices such as sprint planning, daily stand-ups, sprint reviews, and retrospectives. I also want to continue using user stories and a product backlog to keep development focused on the highest-priority requirements.

## What does it mean to be a good team member in software development?

Being a good team member means communicating clearly, completing assigned work, helping others when needed, and being willing to accept feedback. Software development requires collaboration, so team members need to respect different ideas and work together to solve problems. A good team member should also take responsibility for their work and communicate early when they encounter a problem that could affect the team. Agile development showed me that successful software development depends not only on technical skills but also on communication, flexibility, and teamwork.
